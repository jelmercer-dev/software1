How to play

Use file  "launch.py" to launch the game. 
On mac: right-click on the file --> open with --> Python Launcher
On windows: 

Find the five Infinity Stones hidden across the Renewal Village, Riverlands,
Living Forest, Knowledge Highlands, and Unity Commons. Each location contains
one stone.

Enter your name to start a new game or continue your saved game.
Enter w to move, then type one of the available destinations.
Enter e to collect the Infinity Stone in your location.
Enter i to view your inventory, r to rest, or v to hear a riddle.
Your progress is saved automatically. Collect all five stones to transform the
world and help it work toward all 17 United Nations Sustainable Development
Goals. Enter lopeta to quit.
